What Moved the Market

Korean equities experienced a severe correction on November 16, 2026, as the KOSPI index dived 3.59% to close at 5,100.50 and the KOSDAQ index slumped 4.86% to 665.65. Heavy selling pressure was concentrated in technology and semiconductor heavyweights, weighing down the entire market.

Foreign and institutional investors led the net selling. In the KOSPI market, foreigners dumped 13,598 billion won and institutions sold 4,600 billion won, while individual investors net bought 13,244 billion won. A similar trend was seen in the KOSDAQ, where foreign and institutional net outflows reached 2,087 billion won and 1,538 billion won respectively.

Semiconductor and electronic equipment sectors bore the brunt of the decline. Samsung Electronics dropped 5.05% to 195,600 won and SK Hynix plunged 8.14% to 1,117,000 won. Major suppliers like SK Square also dropped 10.24%. In contrast, select defensive and reopening names such as airlines and insurance saw modest gains.

Despite the dramatic slump in local equities, the foreign exchange market showed resilience. The USD/KRW rate edged down 0.2% (-2.7 won) to 1,335.30, indicating that the local currency strengthened slightly against the dollar despite the domestic stock market rout.
