What Moved the Market

The KOSPI index advanced 4.34% (204.47 points) to close at 4,915.68, while the KOSDAQ index skyrocketed 8.13% (53.28 points) to end at 708.99. Both markets experienced strong upward momentum, fueled by easing concerns over AI-related adjustments linked to Nvidia.

Foreign investors returned aggressively, net purchasing 11,506 billion won in the KOSPI market and 3,017 billion won in the KOSDAQ market. Institutions also supported the rally with net purchases of 2,356 billion won in KOSPI and 5,871 billion won in KOSDAQ, offsetting individual net selling.

Large-cap tech stocks led the advance, with Samsung Electronics gaining 4.67% to 179,300 won and SK Hynix surging 5.32% to 1,109,000 won. Sector performance was led by life science tools and services, biotechnology, and telecommunication equipment.

The foreign exchange market saw the won strengthen as the USD/KRW exchange rate fell 8.8 won (-0.65%) to close at 1,335.2, falling below 1,400 won due to month-end exporter dollar selling and the broader equity market rally. Overseas indices also posted gains, including the Shanghai Composite and Hang Seng.
