What Moved the Market

Heavy foreign net selling dominated the session, with offshore investors dumping 14,099 billion won on the KOSPI and 882 billion won on the KOSDAQ. Institutions bought 5,292 billion won and retail investors added 4,049 billion won on the main bourse, but failed to cushion the foreign exit.

Market bellwethers retreated across the board, led by Samsung Electronics (-3.10%), Samsung Electronics Preferred (-5.52%), and SK Hynix (-1.61%). LG Energy Solution slid 4.08%, and Samsung Electro-Mechanics fell 4.59%, tracking cautious sentiment around global semiconductor and technology leaders.

Sector performance skewed heavily negative, with Electricity Utilities (-6.04%), Electrical Equipment (-4.82%), and Non-ferrous Metals (-4.60%) leading the drop. Conversely, defensive niches showed resilience, led by Healthcare Technology (+2.18%) and Department Stores (+1.95%).

Regional sentiment offered little support as Japan's Nikkei 225 lost 1.34% and Hong Kong's Hang Seng fell 1.03%. Meanwhile, the USD/KRW rate rose 0.4 won to 1,336.9 won, reflecting modest won weakness alongside a 5.12% yield on the US 10-year Treasury.
